02 / READ THE BOUNDS

What the scorecard says

Bounds treat every unknown rating as anywhere from 0 to 5. They are not probabilities or statistical confidence intervals. Options stay in input order.

    KEEP THE REASONING WITH THE NUMBERS

    Take the discussion with you.

    Inspect export before downloading

    Exports include option names, weights, ratings, and notes. Temporary what-if tests are omitted. Review them before sharing. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

    WEIGHTS ARE RELATIVE

    No need to total 100.

    Use whole-number weights from 0–100. Scorefold normalizes them. A weight of 8 matters twice as much as 4.

    RANGES ARE CONDITIONAL

    Clear lead, within this model.

    An option has a clear lead only when its lower bound is above every other option's upper bound.

    JUDGMENT STAYS WITH YOU

    A scorecard, not an oracle.

    The tool doesn't verify evidence, choose criteria, or make your decision. Use the numbers to ask better questions.
